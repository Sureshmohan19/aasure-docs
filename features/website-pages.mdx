---
title: Website pages
description: Manage draft pages, recover deleted pages, and publish changes to your live website.
---

Website pages are edited in Studio. Your draft can differ from the live site until you publish it.

## Move a page to Trash

In the website editor, open **Menu**, choose the page, and select **Delete**. The page leaves your draft immediately. Your live site continues to serve its last published version until you publish again.

Studio keeps deleted pages in **Page Trash** for 30 days. After that, they are permanently removed.

## Restore a page

Open **Menu → Page Trash** in the website editor, then select **Restore** beside the page. The page returns to your draft. Add it to your menu separately if you want it in the navigation, then publish to update the live site.

If another active page already uses the same URL path, change that page's path before restoring. A page built with a different website template cannot be restored into the current template; its **Restore** button is disabled.

<Note>
  Deleting or restoring a page changes the draft. Publish when you want visitors to see the change.
</Note>
