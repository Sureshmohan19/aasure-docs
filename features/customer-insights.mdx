Open **Customers → Insights** to see how your customer book is changing and who may need attention. Select a date range for movement and recent appointment measures; the report labels measures that use the full customer history.

## What the numbers mean

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Net customer change | Customers added during the selected period minus customers moved to trash during it. |
| Second-visit rate | Share of customers with a completed first visit who completed another visit within 90 days. Uses full history. |
| Forward cover | Upcoming bookings divided by upcoming bookings plus completed appointments in the selected period. |
| Recorded booking spend | Money captured for bookings at Diary checkout, minus refunds, across full history. Voided sales are excluded. |
| At-risk customer spend | Historical recorded booking spend of all customers currently in the at-risk health group. This is context for follow-up, not predicted revenue. |

Spend uses the business currency and appears after a paid checkout. The report does not convert payments in another currency or infer payment from a service's listed price. A fully refunded checkout contributes zero. Diary checkout currently records **Cash** or **Other**; an unpaid booking has no recorded spend.

## Customer health and follow-up

The health map groups customers by recent activity and future bookings. A future booking places a customer in **Booked**. Without one, recent activity places them in **Active**; older activity moves them through **Due now**, **At risk**, and **Lapsed** according to the business's customer settings. Completed visits in the past count toward return measures; cancelled appointments and future visits do not.

**Who is slipping away** lists the highest-spend at-risk customers. Select a name to open that customer's panel. The list shows up to five people, while the at-risk spend total includes the entire at-risk group.

## Read the other sections

- **Return behaviour** shows second visits, repeat visits, rebooking, and recovery after cancelled or missed appointments. Reference markers help you compare rates.
- **Visit interval drift** compares the latest monthly gap between completed visits with the earlier observed gaps. It waits for real visit intervals instead of inventing a baseline.
- **Acquisition quality** groups customers by recorded source and shows repeat rate and average recorded booking spend per customer.
- **Customer cohorts** group customers by the month they were added and show the share with a completed visit at or after 3, 6, and 12 months. A dash means that cohort has not reached the milestone yet.
- **Data quality** highlights missing birthdays, contact reach, and service prices. Service prices describe potential booking value; recorded spend always comes from checkout.
